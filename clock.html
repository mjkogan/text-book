Text Book

  • pg. 1: one word at a
    A serial/parallel reading of Wind is Blowing by Chika Sagawa. Use any of the arrow keys to move through the text.
  • pg. 2: time
    One second poems, presented according to the hours, minutes, and seconds of the current time.
  • pg. 3: blackbirds
    A hypertext presentation of Thirteen Ways of Looking at a Blackbird by Wallace Stevens. Follow the links to move through the text.
  • pg. 4: type
    Type any five letters to generate a five-word sentence. Press the 'Enter' key to clear the display.
